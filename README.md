# case-study-2

Introduction

The CEO  for brighttv has set a clear strategic objective for this financial year which is to  significantly grow the company’s subscription base. Achieving this goal requires a deep understanding of customer behaviour, preferences, and value drivers. 

Aim

Is to generate actionable insights that will support the Customer Value Management (CVM) team in identifying opportunities to attract, retain, and maximize the value of subscribers.

Insights

Viewership is highest at night, making it the peak period when most users are active. Engagement begins to rise in the evening, which is the second strongest time slot. The afternoon shows moderate but steady activity, while the morning has the lowest viewership, likely due to work or school commitments.
March is a peak month.


Recommendation

Prioritize prime-time (Evening & Night) content schedule high-demand shows, premieres, and exclusive content during these peak hours to maximize engagement and subscriptions.
Introduce targeted promotions at night use push notifications, discounts, or featured content during peak time to convert active viewers into subscriber
Maximize weekday engagement (core strength)Schedule premium content, new episodes, and key releases during weekdays leverage habitual viewing with daily or weekly series
Improve weekend engagement Introduce special weekend content (movies, live events, binge-worthy series)Run weekend promotions or discounted bundles to attract more viewersLeverage High-Performing Events
 In April Promote top programmes more aggressively.
